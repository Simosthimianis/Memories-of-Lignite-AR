# Credits & Source Notes

## Project context
This project was developed as an academic digital-application project focused on the *Memories of Lignite* monument in Ptolemaida, Greece.

## Research / source material
The project documentation identifies the following source types:
- Personal / family oral testimony from a former DEI worker
- On-site observation of the *Memories of Lignite* monument / park
- Photographic material of the monument
- Local publications and archival material related to lignite, DEI and Ptolemaida
- Course material and official Unity, Vuforia and Mapbox documentation

## AI-assisted material
AI tools were used for ideation, draft voice-over lines and indicative visual mockups. ElevenLabs was used for text-to-speech narration. AI-assisted images were used as design mockups and are **not presented as authentic historical photographs or documents**.

## Portfolio note
This repository is intended to document the design and prototype process. Any third-party or archival material remains the property of its respective rights holders.
