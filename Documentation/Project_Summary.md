# Project Summary

## Title
Memories of Lignite

## Format
Mobile location-based Augmented Reality cultural experience

## Location
Ptolemaida, Greece — *Memories of Lignite* monument / park

## Purpose
The project aims to reinterpret a static public monument as an active digital carrier of memory. The experience focuses on the historical, social and cultural importance of lignite, DEI and mine workers for Ptolemaida.

## Narrative Structure
The experience follows three linked moments:

1. **The Beginning** — the worker's first day in the mine.
2. **The People of DEI** — daily life, shifts, colleagues and solidarity.
3. **Today** — memory, transition and reflection after the lignite era.

## Core Experience
Users scan different image targets to activate the corresponding AR section. Each point combines interface text, voice narration, imagery and audio in a museum/documentary-style presentation.

## Design Intent
The project deliberately avoids presenting the topic only through factual information. The central narrative device is the voice of a person who lived through the lignite era, giving the experience the tone of oral history and personal memory.
