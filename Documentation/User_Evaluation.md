# User Evaluation

## Method
The prototype's basic flow was evaluated with 3 participants. Participants interacted with the three AR points and commented on scanning, narration, visual fit, clarity and the relationship between the experience and Ptolemaida's lignite history.

## Key Findings

### What worked well
- The three-part progression was easy to understand.
- The chronological sequence helped users follow the story.
- Voice narration was particularly well received because it made the experience feel personal and testimonial.
- The UI panels were considered concise and helpful.

### Areas for improvement
- Improve image/text clarity and typography for outdoor use.
- Improve voice-over volume and audio quality in outdoor conditions.
- Add replay controls for narration and complete AR points.
- Provide clearer onboarding/scanning instructions for less experienced users.

## Design Interpretation
The evaluation suggests that the strongest part of the experience is the combination of AR interaction with personal oral-history-style narration. Future iterations should preserve that narrative strength while improving accessibility, readability and control.
